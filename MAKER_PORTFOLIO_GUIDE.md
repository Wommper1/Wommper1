# Adding real-world work to this portfolio

This is a framework for documenting actual work, not a list of completed or released projects. Keep private addresses, family details, and sensitive locations out of public photos and notes.

## One portfolio, several ways in

- **Digital:** apps and tools; link source, version, requirements, a real demo and testing limits.
- **Physical:** woodworking, modifications and equipment experiments; show the need, materials, process, result and what remains unknown. Personal experiments are not licensed-trade credentials.
- **Creative:** a small selection of your own photographs or media, with purpose, tools, your role, and permission to show recognizable people or client material.
- **Exploration:** a place-based question, sources, photos taken from permitted locations, confidence labels and what you learned. Avoid sensitive-site coordinates and private-property directions.
- **Professional:** a permitted, anonymized example explaining a customer need and your contribution. No employer names, private client data, invented outcomes or sales statistics.

## A repeatable capture routine

1. Write one sentence: “I wanted to find out whether…”
2. Take a before, process and finished photo where practical; a missing before photo is fine—say so.
3. Note tools/materials, what you actually did, what failed, and what you observed.
4. Record a short demo only if it adds evidence. Use captions/transcript for spoken material.
5. Complete [the case-study worksheet](CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md), then choose the best few images.
6. Review privacy, ownership, permissions and claims before linking from the main profile.

Keep entries here or in the company field notes until a project has enough substance for its own repository. No empty category repositories are needed.
