# Case study: [project name]

Use this worksheet for a real project. Replace prompts before presenting it as completed work. Do not publish private data or a project that has not been authorized for release.

## At a glance
- Date/version and status: idea / prototype / usable / released / archived
- Audience and platform:

- Source, release or sample:

## The challenge
What specific task or opportunity started this? Who might use the result? Avoid an invented customer or business impact.

## The investigation
What did you inspect, compare, measure or learn? Link sources and dated notes. Distinguish what you remember from a recorded observation.

## The approach
What did you choose to attempt, and why? Only describe a decision as historical fact when it is documented or you can confirm it.

## The build
What exists? Show a real screenshot, photograph, recording or source file. Name verified tools, materials and technologies. Credit third-party work.

## The obstacles
Describe a documented failure or limitation, its evidence, what you changed, and whether it was retested. If none is recorded, say so.

## The result
List checks with date, version, environment, method and outcome. Separate observed results, prior documented results and untested claims. Do not turn automated tests into hardware acceptance evidence.

## What comes next
What remains uncertain? What is the next useful experiment? Separate plans from commitments.

## Try it / contribute
Requirements, reproducible small task, expected result, known limits, contribution route and private security-reporting route where applicable.

## Evidence and permissions
Sources, attribution, media permission, licenses already applicable, privacy review and update date. Do not assign new license terms without approval.
